Vector databases are specialized databases designed to store and query high-dimensional vectors (embeddings).
They are a critical component in Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) systems and semantic search applications.
Unlike traditional databases that use exact keyword matching, vector databases use approximate nearest neighbor (ANN) search.
This allows them to find semantically similar content even when words don't match exactly.
Popular vector databases include Chroma, FAISS, Pinecone, Weaviate, and Qdrant.
Embeddings are numerical representations of text (or images) that capture semantic meaning in a dense vector space.
When you store a document, it is first converted to an embedding and then stored alongside the original content.
At query time, the question is also embedded and the most similar document vectors are retrieved.
